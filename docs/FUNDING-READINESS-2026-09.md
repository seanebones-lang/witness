# Witness Funding Readiness — September 2026

Witness may be fundable as open-source infrastructure for evidence provenance,
AI accountability, scientific reproducibility, journalism, archives, and digital
public infrastructure. The project does not yet have external users,
contributors, advisers, pilot outcomes, or independent review. Those are the
next evidence gates.

## Fundable first project

**Witness Reference Protocol and Environmental Evidence Pilot**

Proposed twelve-month outputs:

1. Independently reviewed Epistemic Constitution.
2. Canonical record and serialization specification.
3. Language-neutral conformance fixtures and an independent verifier.
4. Correction, supersession, key rotation, revocation, and compromise records.
5. Structured multidimensional verification results.
6. A raw-source preservation format with retrieval metadata and digests.
7. One small environmental-data importer and guided river-gauge demonstration.
8. Security, privacy, accessibility, archival, and domain review.
9. Contributor documentation and a pilot evaluation report.

The proposal must promise these bounded outputs rather than claim that Witness
will determine truth or become universal infrastructure during the grant.

## Current funding paths

### NLnet — nearest small technical-grant path

NLnet lists a **November 3, 2026 at 12:00 CET** deadline and first grants from
**€5,000 to €50,000**. It supports open software, data, hardware, and standards
for a trustworthy, resilient, human-centered internet:

- <https://nlnet.nl/propose/>
- <https://nlnet.nl/funding.html>

Fit: canonicalization, conformance fixtures, correction records, an independent
verifier, and the reference pilot. Risks: current EU-supported funds generally
need a meaningful European dimension. NLnet requires candid disclosure of
generative-AI assistance and asks applicants to write proposals in their own
words. Witness must disclose its AI-assisted development and proposal process
exactly as requested. Eligibility and fund selection must be confirmed before
substantial application work.

### NSF PESOSE Track 1 — larger 2027 ecosystem path

The current NSF PESOSE solicitation lists a **March 2, 2027** deadline. Track 1
supports up to **$300,000 for one year** of open-source ecosystem scoping and
planning; Tracks 2 and 3 support up to $1.5 million:

- <https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/pesose-pathways-enable-secure-open-source-ecosystems/nsf26-506/solicitation>

Fit: secure open-source infrastructure, governance, community formation, and
research/public-sector collaboration. Current gaps: an eligible submitting
organization and PI, an external user and contributor base, and three to five
required collaboration letters from existing third-party users or contributors.
Witness should treat PESOSE as a target earned through the pilot and review
campaign, not an application-ready claim.

### Open Technology Fund — conditional human-rights path

OTF accepts Internet Freedom Fund proposals on a rolling basis for open-source
technology and research supporting freedom of expression and people facing
repression:

- <https://apply.opentech.fund/>
- <https://www.opentech.fund/funds/internet-freedom-fund/>

Fit requires a real journalism or human-rights partner and a specific problem,
such as preserving edited public releases, tracking corrections safely, or
distinguishing archived observations from generated propaganda. The project
must not invent an internet-freedom use case merely to match the fund.

### Mozilla

Mozilla Open Source Support is on indefinite hiatus and is not accepting
applications. It should not be represented as a current funding opportunity:

- <https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/moss/>

Mozilla-aligned researchers and public-interest technologists may still be
valuable reviewers or sources of introductions.

## People and evidence to recruit

Before a larger application, seek:

- One measurement, scientific-data, or metrology reviewer.
- One archivist, librarian, or digital-preservation reviewer.
- One journalist, public-records practitioner, or newsroom technologist.
- One security or privacy reviewer.
- One standards and interoperability reviewer.
- One affected-community or human-rights reviewer when a domain pilot is chosen.
- One prospective pilot organization willing to test a narrow workflow.

Reviews may be critical and do not need to endorse Witness. Record the reviewer's
scope, material objections, project response, and any permission to quote or name
them.

## Applicant and sustainability options

Do not create a new nonprofit until a real funding route requires it. Evaluate:

- an individual grant;
- NextEleven as a U.S. business applicant;
- a nonprofit fiscal sponsor;
- a university-led collaboration; or
- a later public-benefit nonprofit with multi-stakeholder governance.

The open protocol, schemas, verifier, and conformance fixtures should remain
public. Sustainable services may include hosted operation, deployment,
integration, evidence migration, audits, training, and support.

## Immediate campaign

1. Resolve the GitHub billing lock and obtain a green CI run.
2. Publish scoped expert-review issues and invite five to eight reviewers.
3. Record reviews, objections, and changes without implying endorsement.
4. Secure one small, public, reversible pilot partner.
5. Build the source-preserving environmental reference story.
6. Prepare a small technical-grant budget and eligibility check.
7. Use the resulting contributors, users, and letters to assess NSF PESOSE for
   March 2027.

## Evidence standard

Funding, application, award, implementation, external review, adoption, and
impact are separate states. This document records possible routes and current
requirements. It does not claim that Witness has applied for or received funds.
