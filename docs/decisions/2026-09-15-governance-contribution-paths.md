# Decision: create durable routes for review notes, concerns, and dissent

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Review date:** 2026-09-15
- **Constitution version:** 0.1.0
- **Decision maker:** Sean McDonnell, sole current maintainer

## Problem and evidence

The contribution process invited short expert review but made the ten-part proposal shape appear to be the general route for material input. It permitted minority reports without a location or decision-linking duty and described an affected-person route without an operational entry point. The linked community-governance review identified these gaps; it is owner-supplied-unverified.

## Affected people and consultation

People with domain expertise, people affected by a deployment, reviewers who do not write code, and dissenting contributors may be excluded by a code-centered process. No external consultation or affected-person testing has yet occurred.

## Decision

Create documented paths for review notes, change proposals, and affected-person concerns. Publish minority-report and decision-record shelves. Require a public decision record when a material domain, privacy, or security objection is refused. Require material generative-tool disclosure and make sole-maintainer status and non-endorsement explicit.

## Guarantee and limits

The project will preserve a route and durable pointer for these inputs. This does not make reviews binding, establish anonymity, create a review quorum, provide a confidential case-management system, create an appeal body, or grant deployment approval.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep one proposal path: rejected because it burdens short, non-code criticism.
- Claim a future governance body now: rejected because no such body exists.
- Treat compatible forks as appeal: rejected for people affected by a deployment.

## Compatibility, migration, and privacy

Documentation and issue-template change only; no schema, API, signature, or stored-record migration. The public concern form warns against sensitive facts.
