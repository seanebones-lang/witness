INFERRED

Consensus Inference

Claim: The two local Vina replicates (seeds 101, 202) produce correlated docking scores (r = 0.885) exceeding the consensus threshold (r ≥ 0.80), showing bounded score agreement for this six-compound example, without establishing biological binding affinity.

Premises: 12 observed docking measurements (6 compounds × 2 replicates), each from separate AutoDock Vina runs with different random seeds but identical binding-site parameters.

Methodology: Pearson correlation between replicate score vectors. Outlier detection via IQR on per-compound absolute differences.

Uncertainty: 0.115 (= 1 − rounded r); a heuristic field, not a confidence interval

Falsifiers (What Would Change This Conclusion)

Pending
Third replicate falsification: A third independent Vina replicate with a different seed produces docking scores that correlate with the first two at r < 0.80.
Pending
Experimental contradiction: Wet-lab binding assays (ITC, SPR) for the six compounds contradict the predicted rank order from consensus docking scores.
Completed-Falsified
Incorrect center falsification: Using the earlier incorrect binding-site center yields r < 0.80 or reversed rank order — already observed in the failed attempt.
