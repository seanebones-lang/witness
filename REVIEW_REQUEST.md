# Request for independent review: Proof A

Witness is asking for a skeptical, bounded technical review of **EXP-001 Proof A**, a local computational-infrastructure demonstration built with Humanity Grid. The public proof state is frozen in the annotated `proof-a-v1` tag in both repositories.

## What Proof A demonstrates

- A local computational docking workflow produced two specified score vectors.
- The preserved comparison reports Pearson `r = 0.8849788400337059` under a local pass threshold of `r >= 0.80`, with one explicit outlier.
- A fresh replay can submit 12 Observed records, two Inferred records, and one Generated hypothesis to Witness through its public local REST contract.
- The inferred consensus record explicitly links its twelve premises and names falsifiers.
- Review artifacts have hashes, sizes, source notes, and a repeatable local verification path.

## What Proof A does **not** demonstrate

It does not establish a drug candidate, biological activity, clinical value, docking enrichment, an independently executed computation, independent custody, source identity, external endorsement, or scientific truth. It is not a volunteer-computing service and is not suitable for clinical, regulatory, or consequential decision making.

## The request

We ask reviewers to assess one or more of these questions:

1. Can the documented local review path be followed and its stated checks be reproduced?
2. Are the scientific and epistemic claims bounded accurately?
3. Does the record model preserve the distinction between source material, local observations, inferences, generated hypotheses, and unresolved uncertainty without overstating what it proves?
4. What pre-registered criteria, controls, domain input, or independent work would be necessary for a credible Proof B?

Negative findings, inability to reproduce, design objections, and disagreement are welcome contributions. Review is not endorsement. A reviewer may publish a critique, file an issue, or decline to be named.
