# Known limitations before independent review

This list is intentionally public so a reviewer does not need to discover every limitation before offering useful criticism. It is not a claim that the list is complete.

## Proof A scientific limits

- EXP-001 is a local, retrospective computational-infrastructure demonstration, not a prospective discovery experiment.
- Two Vina score vectors and their correlation do not establish docking accuracy, enrichment, binding affinity, biological activity, or efficacy.
- The `r >= 0.80` agreement rule is local to this demonstration and has not been independently pre-registered or validated as a general scientific threshold.
- The six-compound selection, receptor preparation, scoring setup, and outlier rule need external domain review before being used for a scientific claim.
- The package preserves local outputs but does not prove independent execution, hardware identity, toolchain integrity, or custody before local capture.

## Witness protocol limits

- Append-only storage and content identifiers make later changes detectable in the stored system; they do not prove that a record is true, complete, independently witnessed, correctly interpreted, or created by a claimed person.
- Current signing verifies a submitted payload under a key. It does not create a complete public-key identity, authorization, revocation, timestamping, or institutional custody system.
- The current model does not yet represent every measurement semantic needed for a conforming instrument observation. The USGS reference package deliberately stops before claiming one.
- The system has not been independently security-reviewed, scaled, deployed as a multi-tenant service, or tested as a long-term archive.

## Governance and rights limits

- Project-origin notices and Git history are attribution evidence, not a substitute for employment assignment, contractor agreements, trademark registration, or legal review.
- The root software licenses do not silently relicense upstream datasets, source structures, source identifiers, or third-party tools.
- No external laboratory, institution, funder, standards body, or government agency has endorsed this project.

## What would reduce these limits

An independently designed, pre-registered Proof B; domain review; a clean-room reproduction; explicit source/data rights review; signed producer identity; correction and supersession flows; and public responses to adverse findings.
