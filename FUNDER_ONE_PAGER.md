# Witness + Humanity Grid — Funding Partner Brief

> **This is a request to fund an independently designed and reviewed Proof B.**
> It is not a claim that Proof A produced a scientific discovery, a drug
> candidate, or an independently validated platform.

## The problem

AI-assisted research can produce hypotheses, calculations, summaries, and confident conclusions faster than researchers can inspect their basis. The failure is not generated language by itself. It is the collapse of three different things into one record: what was observed, what was inferred, and what was generated.

## The work

**Witness** is an open-source, append-only record system that keeps Observed, Inferred, and Generated material distinct. It preserves stated premises, methodology, uncertainty, falsifiers, corrections, and unresolved objections.

**Humanity Grid** is the first local computational producer and reproducibility demonstration. **Research Scout** is a proposed hypothesis-generation layer; its output remains Generated, not evidence.

## What exists today

Frozen **EXP-001 Proof A** is tagged `proof-a-v1` in both repositories. The checksummed package contains two specified local AutoDock Vina score vectors for a CDK2 example, a preserved agreement calculation with one recorded outlier, and a fresh replay that writes **12 Observed**, **2 Inferred**, and **1 Generated** record to Witness.

Proof A demonstrates preserved local artifacts and a replayable Grid-to-Witness record path. It does **not** demonstrate docking accuracy, biological activity, efficacy, a drug candidate, independent execution, external validation, or scientific discovery.

## The funded next step

Fund a six-month **Proof B**: an independently defined and reviewed computational-research experiment.

The work will produce:

1. A domain-defined question, method, source-rights inventory, and acceptance criteria written before execution.
2. A reproducible package with preserved inputs, tool versions, parameters, raw outputs, deviations, and failures.
3. A Witness record that separates observations, inferences, generated material, corrections, and unresolved objections.
4. At least one independent reproduction attempt.
5. A public review and project response whether the result passes, fails, or is inconclusive.

## The ask

We seek a **$100,000 funding partner for six months**, or an equivalent combination of direct support, independent domain-review time, compute, and reproducibility work. We also seek a domain collaborator with the freedom to criticize the method and result publicly.

The money funds a testable process, not a promised outcome.
