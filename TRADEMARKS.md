# Witness naming policy

“Witness” is the project name used by its originator and current maintainer,
Sean McDonnell. This policy applies whether or not a mark has been registered
in a particular jurisdiction.

The MPL 2.0 license grants permission to use the software under its terms. It
does not grant permission to represent a fork, deployment, review, or service
as the official Witness project, or to imply endorsement by Sean McDonnell,
NextEleven LLC, contributors, reviewers, or affected communities.

You may use the name “Witness” factually to identify an unmodified copy or to
describe compatibility, for example: “based on Witness” or “compatible with
Witness.” For a modified fork or service, use a distinct name and make the
relationship clear. Do not use Witness branding in a way likely to confuse a
reasonable user about source, stewardship, review status, scientific validation,
or deployment authority.

This is a project naming policy, not a claim that the name is registered or a
substitute for trademark clearance or legal advice.
