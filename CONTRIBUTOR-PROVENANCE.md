# Contributor provenance and sign-off

Witness accepts contributions under MPL 2.0. Contributors retain their own
copyright unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

Every pull request must include a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off in
each commit:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.org>
```

Use `git commit -s` to add it. By signing off, a contributor certifies that the
submission is their original work or that they have the right to submit it under
the repository license, consistent with the Developer Certificate of Origin
1.1. The maintainer may request source, license, authorship, conflict, or
generative-tool provenance before accepting work.

A contribution, sign-off, review, or acknowledgement is not an endorsement of
Witness, its governance, a scientific result, or a deployment.

Before accepting scientific artifacts, maintainers must record their source,
license or reuse basis, rights limits, and whether the artifact is a fixture,
curated demonstration, or independently reviewed evidence.
