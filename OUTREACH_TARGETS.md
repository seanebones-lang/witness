# Initial independent-review outreach targets

These are organizations or public communities selected for fit with a narrow Proof A review request. Inclusion is not an endorsement, partnership, or claim of contact. Do not mass-email this list. Send a tailored, one-time note through the public route stated by each organization.

| Priority | Target | Best review lane | Why it fits | Public route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Molecular Sciences Software Institute (MolSSI) | Computational molecular science and research software | Its mission includes open-source software and infrastructure for computational molecular science. | `info@molssi.org` — [official site](https://molssi.org/) |
| 2 | Open Molecular Software Foundation / OpenADMET | Molecular scientific software | It supports sustainable open-source molecular-science communities and has a public contact route. | [OpenADMET contact page](https://openadmet.org/about/contact/) |
| 3 | Center for Open Science | Reproducibility and research workflow | Its Open Science Framework supports planning, preregistration, sharing, and reproducibility. | `contact@cos.io` — [official GitHub profile](https://github.com/CenterForOpenScience) |
| 4 | Research Software Alliance (ReSA) | Research software, authorship, and reproducibility | Its community includes work on research software authorship and FAIR research software. | `info@researchsoft.org` — [official contact page](https://www.researchsoft.org/about/contact/) |
| 5 | The Turing Way community | Reproducible and collaborative research practice | It is an open community focused on reproducible, ethical, collaborative research. | `turingway@turing.ac.uk` — [community contact guidance](https://github.com/the-turing-way/the-turing-way) |

## Outreach order

Begin with MolSSI, ReSA, and Center for Open Science. Each asks a different question: molecular-workflow validity, research-software practice, and transparent/reproducible research design. Do not ask any organization to endorse the project, fund it, or vouch for a scientific result in the first message.
