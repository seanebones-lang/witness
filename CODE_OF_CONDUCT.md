# Witness Community Code of Conduct

Witness depends on disagreement that remains rigorous, humane, and safe.
Participation in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
project events requires the conduct below.

## Expected conduct

- Challenge claims, assumptions, methods, and power with evidence.
- Separate criticism of work from judgment of a person.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes visibly.
- Welcome technical, domain, archival, privacy, accessibility, and lived
  experience as different forms of relevant expertise.
- Respect consent, confidentiality, attribution, and the safety of people
  represented in evidence.
- Make space for minority reports and people affected by a proposed system.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention,
deliberate intimidation, impersonation, retaliation, coordinated disruption, and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. So are
knowingly submitting confidential evidence, credentials, private keys, or
personal data to public project spaces.

Disagreement with a maintainer, institution, scientific position, or majority is
not misconduct by itself. Enforcement must address behavior and safety rather
than suppress a good-faith challenge to a claim.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's abuse-reporting controls for content or accounts that violate
GitHub policy. For project-specific conduct reports, contact the maintainer using
a private contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/seanebones-lang). Do not place
sensitive report details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities use the
separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be handled as privately as circumstances allow. The maintainer may
remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban a participant based
on severity, pattern, safety, and willingness to repair harm. Immediate threats
or exposure of private information may be removed before a full review.

Anyone directly involved in a report, including the maintainer, must disclose
that conflict and seek an independent reviewer when one is available. Affected
participants may request a written explanation or reconsideration. Enforcement
actions will be documented publicly at an aggregate level when doing so does not
expose a reporter or affected person.

The present single-maintainer enforcement structure is a known governance limit.
Before high-consequence deployment, Witness must establish an independent
reporting and appeal route.
