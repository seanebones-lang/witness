# Independent-review log

This log records external review activity after a reviewer has consented to
public attribution. It is a record of review and project response, not a list of
endorsements.

## Recording rule

When a response arrives, preserve the original response or link to its public
location, record whether the reviewer consented to attribution, and distinguish
their factual findings from the project’s response. Do not rewrite an adverse
finding into a summary that changes its meaning. If a reviewer requests private
handling, retain only a minimal nonpublic contact record and do not publish it.

## Disposition labels

- **Accepted:** a specific issue, correction, or design change is linked.
- **Partly accepted:** state the accepted and unresolved portions.
- **Contested:** state the disagreement and next evidence needed.
- **Not reproducible:** preserve the steps and environment; do not dismiss it
  without a reproducible counter-result.
- **No action yet:** the finding is visible but awaits evaluation.
