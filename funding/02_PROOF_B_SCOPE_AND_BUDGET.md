# Proof B scope, six-month workplan, and budget

## Funding request

**Request:** $100,000 for six months.

**Purpose:** establish whether a domain-defined computational workflow can be independently inspected, reproduced, criticized, and corrected when the evidence record explicitly separates observations, inferences, and generated material.

This is a development and evaluation budget. It does not purchase a scientific conclusion, a favorable review, drug-discovery output, clinical validation, institutional endorsement, or adoption commitment.

## Workstreams and acceptance evidence

| Workstream | Months | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Independent design | 1 | Domain collaborator selected; research question, source inventory, method, success/failure criteria, and conflicts disclosed before execution. |
| 2. Witness Protocol v1 slice | 1–2 | Versioned producer/evidence envelope, validation rules, conformance fixtures, and an explicit unsupported-state response. |
| 3. Authenticated and replayable ingest | 2–3 | Signed producer identity prototype, key lifecycle limits documented, tamper test, fresh-database replay, and no fabricated success state. |
| 4. Proof B execution package | 2–4 | Pinned toolchain, parameters, inputs, source terms, raw outputs, manifests, and reproducible local instructions. |
| 5. Independent reproduction | 4–5 | A second person or organization attempts the documented process and publishes the result, environment, deviations, and limitations. |
| 6. Review, correction, and publication | 5–6 | Published review, visible project response, unresolved-findings list, correction/supersession scenario, and final report. |

## Proposed budget

| Category | Amount | What it pays for |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Founder engineering and project delivery | $48,000 | Six months of protocol, replay tooling, package, documentation, and response work. |
| Domain collaborator | $18,000 | Design review, benchmark/domain selection, method review, interpretation boundaries, and final critique. |
| Independent reproducibility review | $12,000 | Clean reproduction attempt, environment notes, findings, and review report. |
| Compute, storage, backups, and tooling | $8,000 | Reproducible execution, artifact preservation, test environments, and operational costs. |
| Security and provenance hardening | $8,000 | Signing/identity prototype, threat-model review, tamper tests, correction/supersession work. |
| Documentation, accessibility, and dissemination | $6,000 | Reviewer materials, publication-quality package, technical editing, and open review support. |
| **Total** | **$100,000** | **Six-month Proof B program** |

Amounts are planning estimates, not quotations. A partner may substitute direct support for a line item only if the contribution, independence, conflict, and deliverable are recorded.

## Explicit non-goals

- No claim of biological, clinical, diagnostic, or regulatory value.
- No claim that generated hypotheses are evidence.
- No production public volunteer-computing network.
- No collection of person-concerning data.
- No claim that signing, hashing, timestamps, or append-only storage proves truth, identity, custody, or independent review.
- No obligation for a reviewer or collaborator to reach a favorable conclusion.

## Decision at month six

The funded work produces a decision record:

- **Continue:** a domain collaborator and reproduction attempt show a valuable, reproducible use case with manageable gaps.
- **Revise:** the record model or execution package needs defined changes before another domain.
- **Stop or narrow:** the independent work shows that the approach is not useful or not supportable for the selected domain.

A negative result is a successful completion of the funding obligation if it is preserved and reported accurately.
